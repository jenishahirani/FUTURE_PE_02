# AI UGC Ad Content Pack – Lipstick

## 1. Project Overview

Product: Matte Black Lipstick
Content Type: UGC-Style Short-Form Video Ads
Platforms: Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook
Goal: Create engaging product-focused content using AI-generated UGC ad scripts, hooks, CTAs, and captions.

The content focuses on product appearance, texture, application, packaging, and visual appeal without making unsupported product performance claims.

---

## 2. Target Audience

- Makeup and beauty enthusiasts
- Young adults interested in lipstick and cosmetics
- Social media users who discover beauty products through short-form videos
- Customers looking for visually appealing makeup products

---

## 3. Main Content Angle

The advertisement uses a short-form UGC style.

The video begins with an attention-grabbing hook, introduces the lipstick, shows close-up product visuals, highlights the texture and packaging, and ends with a clear call to action.

"Take a look at this matte black lipstick. The packaging, color, and smooth visual texture make it perfect for a bold beauty-focused look."

"Start with a close-up of the lipstick tube, then reveal the lipstick bullet. Show the texture and color with a slow macro camera movement."

15–20 seconds

## Visual Style
- Macro product shots
- Smooth camera movements
- Soft studio lighting
- Clean beauty background

# 8. Problem–Solution–CTA Structure

### Problem
Customers may find it difficult to choose a lipstick based only on a product image.

### Solution
Use short-form UGC content to show the lipstick from multiple angles, including packaging, texture, color, and swatch.

### CTA
Encourage viewers to explore, save, or check out the product.

# 12. Prompt Engineering Logic

The content was generated using a structured prompt approach.

### Input

Product:
Matte black lipstick

Platform:
Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts / Facebook

Content Style:
UGC-style beauty advertisement

Goal:
Create short-form product-focused advertising content.

### Prompt Structure

1. Define the product.
2. Define the target audience.
3. Define the platform.
4. Create multiple attention-grabbing hooks.
5. Identify a simple customer problem.
6. Present the product as the visual solution.
7. Create a short UGC-style script.
8. Add product demonstration directions.
9. Add a clear CTA.
10. Generate platform-specific captions.
11. Keep the content concise and suitable for short-form video.

---

# 13. Master Prompt Used

You are a UGC content marketing assistant.

Create a short-form UGC advertisement for a matte black lipstick.

Target audience:
Young adults and beauty enthusiasts who discover cosmetic products through social media.

Platforms:
Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and Facebook.

Create:

1. Five different attention-grabbing hooks.
2. Three complete UGC-style ad scripts.
3. Each script must follow Hook → Problem → Solution → Product Demonstration → CTA.
4. Provide suggested video duration.
5. Provide visual directions for each script.
6. Create five CTA variations.
7. Create five social media captions.
8. Adapt the content for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and Facebook.
9. Keep the tone natural, engaging, concise, and suitable for short-form content.
10. Avoid unsupported product performance claims.
11. Focus on product appearance, packaging, texture, color, and visual presentation.
